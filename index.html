How it works

Add a song

Tap ＋ Add and paste chords and lyrics. Check it, then save.

Change the key

Use − + or pick a key and capo. Your choice saves itself.

Play

Tap ▶ Play for the big dark view. Tap the right or left side to turn the page, or the middle to start and pause scrolling. Scrolling is paced to the song's tempo and length, and − + nudge it. A Bluetooth pedal works too, and moves to the next song at the end.
